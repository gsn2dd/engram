Engram Capture — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 August 2026

Engram Capture does not collect your data. There is no Engram server, no account, and no third party. The extension sends your conversations to one place: an endpoint address that you type in yourself, on a server you control.

What the extension reads

When enabled, Engram Capture reads the text of conversations on the pages you are already viewing, on these sites only:

  • claude.ai
  • chatgpt.com
  • chat.openai.com

It reads the prompt and the reply, the page title, and the page URL. It does not read any other site, and it injects no code anywhere else.

Where that text goes

To the HTTPS endpoint you configure on the extension's options page, using a bearer token you also supply, and nowhere else. If you configure no endpoint, nothing is ever transmitted.

The developer operates no collection service and receives none of your data. There is no analytics, no telemetry, no tracking, no advertising, and no error-reporting service.

What is removed before sending

Before any text leaves your browser, the extension strips credential-shaped strings — API keys, bearer tokens, and private keys. This is a safety net, not a guarantee: treat it as a second line of defence rather than a reason to paste secrets into a chat window.

What is stored in your browser

Using chrome.storage, on your machine only: your endpoint URL, your bearer token, your contributor name, and your per-site on/off toggles. The alarms permission is used solely to retry an upload that did not complete before a tab closed.

Data sharing and sale

  • Your data is not sold to anyone.
  • Your data is not transferred for any purpose unrelated to the extension's single purpose.
  • Your data is not used to determine creditworthiness or for lending.

Deleting your data

The developer holds nothing to delete. Captured conversations live on your own server; remove them there. To clear the extension's local settings, remove the extension from Chrome.

Permissions, and why each is needed

  • storage — saves your endpoint, token and toggles locally.
  • alarms — retries an interrupted upload.
  • host access to the three AI sites — reading conversation text on exactly those sites is the extension's entire function.
  • optional host access — requested only for the endpoint address you enter, so the extension may post to it. Granted by you at the moment you save your settings.

Contact
